# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Indicative open-source community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical criticism
- Focusing on evidence, security accuracy, and what is best for the community
- Respecting differing viewpoints and operational security experiences

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information or real credentials without explicit permission
- Intentionally submitting malicious payloads or backdoors outside clearly labeled security test fixtures

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
